CaseFlow AI Product Overview

CaseFlow AI is a planned AI-assisted warrant and case-review workflow for serious FiveM roleplay servers. It is built around structured staff review, configurable rules, audit logs, and human final control.

Join Beta Waitlist Email CaseFlow Labs

Planned features

  • Warrant request intake for suspect details, charges, evidence notes, and probable-cause summaries.
  • Configurable server statute book for roleplay law, review thresholds, and evidence expectations.
  • Structured recommendation output: approve, deny, or request more information.
  • Human override workflow for admins, judges, and authorized staff.
  • Discord webhook notifications and audit logs for review history.
  • Staff permissions for submission, review, override, configuration, and case-history access.

Compatibility

  • QBCore and Qbox are the first planned server-framework targets.
  • ESX support is planned after beta feedback.
  • Self-hosted / BYOK deployment is the initial target model.
  • An optional hosted AI relay is planned for server owners who prefer managed infrastructure.

AI and privacy notes

  • AI is used as decision support, not as an automatic judge or final authority.
  • Inputs are expected to be server-owner-provided text and configuration.
  • Example inputs include statutes, evidence notes, charges, and probable-cause summaries.
  • CaseFlow AI is not designed to scrape marketplace content or train on marketplace assets.
  • BYOK support is planned so server owners can control supported AI-provider usage where available.

Support policy

  • Private beta support will prioritize serious RP communities and server owners first.
  • Planned support includes setup guidance, configuration review, and documentation updates.
  • Post-purchase support is planned for marketplace release.
  • Contact: hello@caseflowlabs.store

Compliance notes

  • No gambling, crypto, loot boxes, NFTs, virtual currency sales, or pay-to-win mechanics.
  • No third-party asset resale.
  • No training on marketplace content.
  • AI processes server-owner-provided text and configuration only.
  • Human staff retain final authority over all warrant and case-review decisions.
