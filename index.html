Product preview

A calmer path from RP incident to staff decision

CaseFlow AI is designed to help police, staff, admins, and judges turn roleplay case material into a consistent review package.

01

Officer or staff submits a warrant request

Suspect details, requested charges, evidence notes, and probable-cause text are captured in one workflow.

02

CaseFlow checks configurable server statutes

The review compares the request against the server owner’s statute book, permissions, and configured review rules.

03

Result: approve, deny, or request more evidence

The output is structured as decision support, not an automatic final ruling.

04

Admin or judge can override

Human staff retain final authority and can record the reason for any manual override.

05

Discord webhook and audit log created

Teams get review visibility, moderation traceability, and a clear record for later staff review.

Compliance and trust

Serious RP tooling without risky marketplace claims

CaseFlow AI is positioned as workflow software for roleplay staff, with human review, auditability, and server-owner configuration at the center.

No prohibited monetization mechanics

No gambling, crypto, loot boxes, NFTs, virtual currency sales, or pay-to-win mechanics.

No third-party asset resale

The product is a workflow and decision-support tool. It is not designed to resell third-party game assets or marketplace content.

No training on marketplace content

AI is used only on server-owner-provided text and configuration, such as statute books, evidence notes, and case summaries.

Human staff final authority

Recommendations are decision support. Admins, judges, and authorized staff can approve, deny, request more information, or override.
