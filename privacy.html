Privacy notes

Privacy Notes

These notes describe the intended privacy posture for the CaseFlow AI pre-launch site and planned product workflow.

Beta emails

The beta waitlist form currently runs without a backend. If you contact CaseFlow Labs by email, your email address and message are used for beta access updates, product questions, and support follow-up.

AI processing

CaseFlow AI is planned to process server-owner-provided text and configuration only. Example inputs may include statute text, charges, evidence notes, and probable-cause summaries supplied by the server owner or authorized staff.

No marketplace scraping or training

CaseFlow AI is not designed to scrape marketplace content, resell third-party assets, or train models on marketplace content.

Human review

AI output is intended as decision support. Human admins, judges, and authorized staff retain final authority over warrant and case-review decisions.
